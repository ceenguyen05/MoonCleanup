## Limits and caveats
- Frame times in this sandbox are irregular (software rendering, 15 to 60 ms steps). I judged motion from per-rAF computed opacity/transform traces, not a locked 60 FPS capture. Curves, monotonicity, no overshoot and the reduced-motion branch are conclusive; exact easing at 16.7 ms resolution is not. The CDP screencast contact sheets (`motion-open-contactsheet-*.png`) are too sparse to show the mid-transition (they caught load frames and the settled state) and were not used for judgment. A frame-locked Tier A pass is still desirable at M1 close.
- No browser-zoom 150% pass beyond the UI-size 130% and DPR 1.5 captures.
- Sandbox 2D canvas backdrop, so scrim legibility over real bright regolith was not re-judged.
