Rail, Dust Vault and Carryall contents don't count toward the cap.

### 9.2 Robot and machine caps (technical limits) ★

Reference mode has no Review-granted permits. Counts are bounded only by fixed **technical caps** that keep the 0.1-CPU
server sim and instanced rendering inside budget [R C4]. The reference has no cap, so this is a browser/hosting
adaptation (status *estimated*; evidence = the max-count performance run, M3 E4). Caps are visible from the start
("Roosts 12 / 24"); at a cap the buy button is disabled with a DOT reason line. No silent slowdown.

| Cap | Value | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roosts (Sandpiper or Plover) | 24 | The former end-state Roost ceiling |
| Albatross | 24 | The former end-state Flock ceiling; still +1 Nest per 3 Albatross |
| Machines (Bead Kilns + Tile Sinterers) | 14 | The former end-state Plant ceiling |
| **Hard ceiling per contract** | **48 robots + 14 machines** | Unchanged; owned by the performance budget |

MVP uses a lower technical cap of 3 Roosts ([scope-tiers §1](scope-tiers.md)). The values are the old end-state caps
(candidate tuning, not reference data); performance-reviewer may lower them until measured. The former Review-by-Review
permit schedule is parked in §24.2.

### 9.3 Reference baseline: robot activity

Default clean-rate multiplier = **1.0** independent of manual-cleaning Effort, player count, shared IP/subnet or
distance to a player. Robot work remains server-authoritative with the same measured budgets. Empty-room
checkpoint/suspension remains a hosting lifecycle rule; it is not a penalty for a connected player who stops sweeping.
The previous On-Shift/Unattended bonus curve is retired from the baseline; no household/subnet activity eligibility gate.
Custom fleet-upgrade/pacing variants may be revisited only as optional additions after reference fidelity.

## 19. How player count affects pacing

1. **Personal output.** N players clean N × 1/(1 + c·(N−1)) as much area. ★ c = **0.05** until the shift owns a Puck Former (16 players crowd one outpost and one Assay Counter with 4 pour slots: 0.57 at 16), then 0.02 (0.77 at 16) (review m3).
2. **Income mix.** All dust pays the same material price whoever cleaned it; larger shifts fund the shared fleet faster only because more hands earn more.
3. **Robots do not scale with headcount** (§9.3). The former On-Shift Bonus is parked in §24.3.
4. **Costs per tier (review m2).** For N members who each want a copy:
   - Shared items (board, suit gear, Cells, robots, machines): × 1.
   - Desk Duster, Crescent Squeegee, Bristle Broom: × 1 (Standard Issue: free to every member once anyone owns one).
   - Lane Broom, Static Rake, Ion Curtain, Assembly Gun, vehicles, vehicle upgrades: list × (1 + 0.4·(N−1)) (Repeat Requisition).
5. **Technical caps** (§9.2) are the same for every shift size; big shifts reach them sooner and then progress through player cleaning, vehicles and production.
6. (Parked) The Shift Docket is in §24.9.1 and the Brightside Monument sink in §24.8.
7. ★ **Uneven attendance.** "co-op-uneven" policy: N members each present a fraction f of contract-active hours (independent sessions). Target row: 4 players at f = 0.5 → Fulfilled in 17–21 contract-active hours (est. 18.5 h; each member ≈9–10 h).

Candidate net effect (pre-reconciliation, included the On-Shift Bonus): 1 → 31.1 h, 2 → 19.8 h, 4 → 15.1 h, 8 → 11.7 h, 16 → 9.0 h (×1.57 / ×2.06 / ×2.66 / ×3.46). Regenerate; without the bonus the co-op speed-up will be smaller (open issue, §23).

## 22. Balance-sim specification (`tools/balance-sim`)

**Purpose.** A deterministic, headless pacing simulator that reads the real `packages/game-data` tables through `@moon-cleanup/game-data`, so code and design can never disagree. It produces time-to-unlock curves for 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 players (and uneven attendance), asserts `pacing-targets.json`, and hunts for exploits. Owned by game-designer; run by the `balance-check` skill and in CI on any change to `packages/game-data`. Everything §17/§18 needs is below; nothing else is assumed. The default policy set is **reference mode**: no §24 system is loaded. A separate `--custom` flag may load §24 data for comparison runs only.

### 22.1 Clock and state

- Event-stepped in **10-s ticks of contract-active time** (time with at least one active player). It never simulates strokes: it uses rates.
- Sun: the contract starts at Boreal sunrise; lit when (t mod 3,600) < 1,800.
- State: wallet; owned items (with paid price and count index); Cell, payload and store levels; per-sell-point flows; Shine (Polished area); cumulative player area A since the current sell point.

### 22.2 Player cleaning

- **Phases.** (a) **Landing Apron** until its 6 plots are Stamped: area 61.4 m² per plot at 0.124 kg/m², 1 pass for every tool, trip d = 30 m, Stamp 50 LM per plot on completion. (b) **Outpost mix** (0.335 kg/m², §1.1 passes) until the player owns a vehicle with cruise ≥6 m/s (the Skiff). (c) **Moon mix** after that.
- **Hand tool area rate** = width × (1 + Broad Strokes + Master Stroke) × 2.2 × (1 + Cadence + Second Wind) × Lane Lock (1.15 if T4) × 0.75 ÷ passes(Depth + Scour + Master Stroke, phase mix) × (1 + Telescoping Pole).
- **Vehicle area rate** = width × (1 + 0.2·Wide Mouth) × (1 + 0.1·Master Stroke) × cruise × (1 + 0.08·Gear Ratio) × WideMouthSpeed (0.92 / 0.85 / 0.78) × 0.8 ÷ passes(Depth, phase mix). Payload = base × (1 + 0.4·Cargo Expander). (Optional §24 Refit adds × (1 + 0.25·Refit) to width and payload under `--custom`.)
- **Mode** = the owned hand tool or vehicle with the highest net rate after duty and night.
- **Duty** = fill ÷ (fill + trip); fill = capacity ÷ (rate × kg/m²).
  - Hand trip before the Puck Former: 2·d ÷ 3.4 m/s + pour, d = 60 m + √(A ÷ π). After the Puck Former: 15 s + pour − 2 s per Puck Magnet level. With Overflow Former: 4 s. Pour = 6 s × (1 − Quick Pour) × (0.5 if Smooth Handoff).
  - Vehicle trip: 100 s × (1 − Fast Tip) × (1 − 0.06·Afterburner) × (1 − 0.03·Throttle Lock) × (0.8 if Smooth Handoff) × (0.95 if Second Wind).
- **Night factor** (player only): 1 − max(0, 0.30 − relief), relief = 0.05 × Crown Lamp level on foot or max(that, Running Lamps 0.10/0.20) in a vehicle; 1.0 with the Lowlight Visor.
- **Shift factor:** N × A_att × 1/(1 + c·(N − 1)); c = 0.05 until a Puck Former is owned, then 0.02; A_att = attendance fraction (1 for all policies except co-op-uneven).

### 22.3 Robots

- **Activity multiplier = 1.0**, always (§9.3): robots run at base rate whenever the room is active, independent of player count or activity. No Effort, On-Shift or Unattended term.
- **Roost** output = Σ unit rate × uptime 0.64 (manual Re-site). Each Re-site costs rail at 2 LM/m × 100 m average.
- **Albatross** output = count × 11 × 0.90.
- Above 97% Shine, Albatross × straggler factor 0.6 (travel to lone plots).
- `--custom` only: Flight Plan terms (Keen Beak, Tailwind, Deep Crop, Wide Wing, Auto-Relocate, Homing straggler factor 0.9 / 1.0) and the On-Shift multiplier from §24.3–§24.4.
- Robots use Moon-mix density (0.424 kg/m²); their rates are net (depth folded in).

### 22.4 Selling, throughput and income

- Flows (kg/h) by source: player, Roost (Puck Stacks), Albatross (Bales). Source changes logistics only, never price.
- **Throughput:** each sell point has capacity rails × 144 t/h (stacks/Bales) or 7.2 t/h (loose pucks); Bale Chutes are unlimited; Assay Counter 4 pour slots. Flow above capacity queues and is reported; the efficient policy adds one rail run (2 LM/m × 200 m) whenever a sell point passes 80%.
- **Routing:** Kilns take dust up to capacity; Tile Sinterers take Beads + dust up to capacity; the rest sells raw at the material price.
- **Income** = Σ sales × payout multiplier + Stamps + Settle + collectibles (per policy). Stamps: player area × 0.85 counts as Polished (credited to the player) and robot area × 1.0 (Flock), with Stamp Premium and Golden Handshake rules.
- **Machines:** baseline throughput × power supply fraction; power bought with the machine at 1,050 LM/kW of load.
- **Costs bundled:** Nest per 3 Albatross; power per machine; rail allowances; Puck Former kit 400; Motor Pool 1,000 with the first vehicle; Assembly Gun before the first build piece.
- **Shine:** Polished area += (0.85 × player area + robot area) × dt. Shine milestones are logged only (no unlocks, no cash).
- **Collectibles:** efficient collects the 6 Boreal Near Parcels at 15/25/32/40/48/55 min (60 s each, 30 LM); explorer adds every collectible on a route; others none. Induction pays no LM.

### 22.5 Purchases

1. **Scripted opening** (mirrors the Induction and Probation checklists): Squeegee → Squeegee Cell I → Bristle → Bristle Cell I → Clip Light → Holster I → Assembly Gun → Puck Former kit → Lane Broom → Motor Pool + Trundle → Static Rake → Broad Strokes I → Roost ×3 → Skiff → Bead Kiln → Bulldust.
2. **Greedy** afterwards. For every candidate the shift may buy now (prerequisites met or bundled into its cost, under its technical cap):
   - Δincome = income(with item) − income(now), from this model at the current tick (items that only change Shine use Δincome = ΔPolished m²/s × 2.28 LM/m², the Moon-mix value of robot-cleaned ground now that Bulk Rate is retired).
   - score = max(0, cost − wallet) ÷ income + cost ÷ Δincome (hours).
   - Pick the lowest score; tie-break by lower cost, then by data-file order. Buy when affordable; recompute every tick.
3. **Non-rate items** (Δincome = 0): Hop Pack, Ballast Soles, Loft, Strong Arm, Holster above L1, old-tool Cells, cosmetics. Bought when wallet ≥ 6× cost (≥ 3× after 4 h); cosmetics never. **Crown Lamp, Lowlight Visor and Running Lamps are income items** (they change the night factor) and go through the greedy score (review m10).
4. Personal purchases scale by §19.4 for the player count.

Other policies: **casual** (skill 0.7 on all rates, buys in list order, Rake before Trundle), **explorer** (all collectibles on routes), **co-op-uneven** (N members, attendance f each), **mvp** (scope-tiers §1 content only: no Puck Former, rails or Assembly Gun; Motor Pool pre-built; Roost bought with money on the pre-built pad, technical cap 3). `--custom` only: **benefits** (all Benefits) and Docket goals for **explorer**.

**Monte Carlo.** `--runs 200 --seed n` jitters skill (0.7–1.1), crowding and uptimes ±10%, and reports P10 / P50 / P90 for every milestone.

### 22.6 Outputs

- `tools/balance-sim/out/report.md`: milestone table per player count (like §18.1), cadence gaps (P50/max), share metrics, throughput report, income and area curves, a spend-by-category table, pass/fail per pacing target, and the **diff against `golden/v2-targets.csv`**.
- `out/curves.csv`: per-tick time, players, wallet, income, player area, Roost area, Albatross area, Shine.
- `out/purchases.csv`.

### 22.7 Exploit hunts (each is a failing test if it triggers)

- Reclaim/Place refund loops yielding LM, including on escalating-price and free items (refund = paid price).
- Settle used as a cleaning tool: Place/Reclaim cycles must clear ≤ the builder's own tool rate and pay ≤ `settle.payFrac` of the dust price.
- Relay or Recall fee abuse.
- Double payment: any kg (puck, Puck Stack, Bale, product) paid more than once, e.g. Gate → Dust Vault → Gate loops.
- Repeat Requisition chains via leave/rejoin.
- Robot rate independence: adding N idle or alt players changes robot output by exactly ×1.0; an empty (suspended) room accrues nothing.
- Technical caps: no purchase path exceeds a cap (including concurrent buys and Reclaim → rebuy); Roost price indexes on the count currently owned.
- Trick payouts above the per-driver or per-shift cap; passengers earning.
- Loose pucks above the caps.
- (`--custom` only) the former On-Shift, Docket, Remote Assay and Review-reward hunts in §24.

**CLI.** `pnpm --filter balance-sim start -- --players 1,2,4,8,16 --policy efficient --runs 1` (default). `--assert` exits non-zero on any red target.

**Acceptance (review B5).** The first implementation (1) implements this spec, (2) serialises the §17/§18.1 rows marked valid for reference mode into `golden/v2-targets.csv`, (3) publishes its own reference-mode results and the diff, and (4) the designer replaces every "regenerate" row in §17/§18 with sim output in the same PR, and explains each valid row that differs by more than 5%. Tuning starts only after that. Every later tuning PR attaches the regenerated report.

## 23. Tuning notes and risks

- **Edges:** first Roost (2.0 h), first production (3.5 h) and first Albatross (≈6.0 h) sit at the upper edge of their windows. Levers: Kiln price −20%, Trundle 1,500, or first Albatross 180,000.
- **Solo completion** (31.1 h candidate) must be regenerated. Expected risks: robot dust at full price snowballs the mid-game faster (Albatross payback ≈4× quicker than under Bulk Rate); without Flight Plan the late fleet tops out at 24 × 11 × 0.9 ≈ 238 m²/s versus the candidate 971 m²/s, which may stretch the clear well beyond 35 h. First levers if out of band: Albatross base rate, Albatross price growth (1.18), technical caps (perf permitting).
- **Co-op targets** (4p ~15 h, 16p 8–10 h) relied on the parked On-Shift Bonus. Without it, co-op speed-up comes only from more hands and shared purchases; the 16-player band will likely be missed. Owner decision whether to restate the co-op bands or pull a co-op lever back in.
- **Purchase gaps:** if the sim shows a solo gap >90 min, check Albatross/Nest price steps and machine power bundles first.
- **Idle accrual:** robots run at full rate while an idle client stays connected (up to the 30-min idle close). Acceptable as reference behavior; balance-reviewer should confirm it can't be extended by reconnect loops.
- **Re-site chores** are one click plus a DOT prompt; Auto-Relocate is parked, so playtests should measure how often players re-site.
- **Server load:** at most 24 Albatross, each clearing a plot every 2–28 s, plus 24 Roosts. That is ≤15 plot updates/s worst case, well inside the 20 Hz sim budget. Loose pucks are capped at 200 per contract.

### 24.3 On-Shift Bonus, Effort and Unattended robots (retired from the baseline; former §9.3)

Robots' clean rate is multiplied by the **On-Shift Bonus**:

| Situation | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| No eligible Custodian has Effort > 0 (**Unattended**) | **×0.5** |
| Partial activity, 0 < A_eff < 1 | ×(0.5 + 0.5·A_eff) |
| A_eff ≥ 1 | **×(1 + 0.6·√(A_eff − 1))** |

| A_eff | 0 | 0.5 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 12 | 16 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multiplier | ×0.50 | ×0.75 | ×1.00 | ×1.60 | ×1.85 | ×2.04 | ×2.34 | ×2.59 | ×2.99 | ×3.32 |

**A_eff = Σ Effort_i** over eligible Custodians, after the anti-farm rules, capped at 16.
- **Effort_i** = min(1, area of dust *you* removed in the last 300 s ÷ (0.4 × your best owned mode's expected area rate × 300 s)). "Dust removed" counts only texels whose level fell, so driving over Polished ground earns nothing. Robot dust never counts.
- **Active** also requires input within the last 30 s and ≥10 m of movement within the last 60 s. Otherwise Effort decays to 0 over 60 s. On Break players count 0.
- **Eligible accounts:** one counted session per account (extra tabs of the same account count 0); account Tenure ≥3 (≈30–40 min of real play, so fresh guest alts don't count); **accounts sharing an IP subnet (/24 IPv4, /56 IPv6) contribute at most 1.0 in total**. The last rule costs households on one connection their co-op robot bonus; they keep every other co-op benefit. It is the owner-directed anti-farm rule.
- **Unattended cap:** the room stays open only while someone has had Effort > 0 within the last 30 min (all room types; aligns with the netcode AFK close). So an idle tab gives at most 30 min of robots at ×0.5, then the contract sleeps.
- **Why this is the solo agency fix:** the sim's baseline (an active solo player) is ×1.00, so every v1 robot number stands. A player who stops cleaning halves the fleet. At every stage solo, ≥50% of Shine progress is player-attributable (own cleaning + the active-vs-Unattended half of robot output; §18.1).
- HUD chip: "Flock ×1.00 · Effort 100%".

### 24.6 Work Front and Flock Formations (former §9.1)

- ★ **Work Front** (Review 1): Albatross choose the nearest non-Polished plot within 800 m of a Work Front, else the nearest anywhere. A Work Front is any active Custodian with Effort ≥50% (§9.3), or a Flare Beacon a Custodian tags "Work Front" (one per Custodian). Work Front changes *where* the flock works, never its rate, so it is pacing-neutral.
